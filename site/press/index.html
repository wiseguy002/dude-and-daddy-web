About the studio

DUDE & DADDY LLC is a father-and-son game studio in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Its first game, Clean Up On Aisle Scream!, is a funny-spooky first-person grocery-store survival game that plays in any web browser, on phones and tablets too.

About the game

Survive a week of night shifts at Fresh Mart. Mop the spills, dodge the managers, and whatever you do, do not look at the freezer. It is spooky and silly, never gory, and made for ages 11 and up.

  • A whole work week of night shifts, from Monday to a big Sunday finale
  • Online leaderboards and co-op play with friends
  • Holiday nights, extra game modes and mini-games, and a store builder
  • Touch controls for phones and tablets
