Our story

A dad, a kid and a mop.

DUDE & DADDY LLC is a father-and-son game studio in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. We made it official on October 17, 2026.

Who is who

Dude

President & Co-Founder. Makes the big calls about what the game is, what is funny and what is too spooky.

Daddy

CEO & Co-Founder. Builds, fixes, publishes and says “let’s ask Dude” a lot.

How we make games

Every big design decision is made together. We ask, we try it, we play it, and we keep what makes us laugh. The rule is funny-spooky and never gory, so it is fine for ages 11 and up.

The first game

Clean Up On Aisle Scream! is a first-person survival game about one week of night shifts at a grocery store. It plays in a web browser, on phones and tablets too, and it keeps growing with new nights, modes and surprises. See what’s new or meet the staff.
