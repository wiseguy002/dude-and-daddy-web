For creators

Streaming and videos.

You can show Clean Up On Aisle Scream! on stream, in videos and in reviews. We would love to watch.

The friendly rules

  • Say the game’s name and link to cleanuponaislescream.com in the description.
  • The game is rated 11 and up. Please do not aim videos for it at young children, and keep the commentary family-friendly.
  • Use our logos and screenshots from the press kit as supplied: no recoloring, stretching or effects.
  • Do not claim the game is yours, and do not sell copies of it.
  • Do not show other players’ real names or personal details. Players use nicknames, so please keep it that way.

Making money from your videos

Ads and sponsorships on your own videos are fine. We have not set up any special deals. If you want to work with us, write to hello@dudeanddaddy.com.

These guidelines are a friendly summary, not a contract, and may be updated.

Handy for streams

Solo play works with no sign-in. For co-op, the host shares a four-letter code. The main menu can save the whole game as one file, and an iPad or phone can add it to the Home Screen.
