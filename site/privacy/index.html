Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026.

The short version

This website keeps one piece of personal information, and only if you give it to us: an email address, when a grown-up joins our news list. We never sell or share it. There are no accounts, no comments, and no chat. We do not use advertising trackers.

What the site stores

  • Your light or dark choice. If you tap the Day shift / Night shift button, your choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device.
  • Which little secrets you found. The site has a few hidden surprises. If you find one, the site remembers it in your own browser. It never leaves your device.
  • Fonts. The fonts are served from this site, not from a font company.

The news list

  • Who it is for. Grown-ups, 18 or older. Kids, please ask a parent to sign up instead.
  • What we keep. Your email address and the date you confirmed. Nothing else. Until you click the link in our confirmation email, we keep nothing about you.
  • What we send. One email to confirm, one to say welcome, and after that only real news: a new game or a big update. No ads.
  • Leaving. Every email has a link to leave. One click removes your address. You can also email us and we will remove it.
  • Who helps us send it. Our emails are delivered by Resend, an email delivery company, from our address at beepboopmail.com. Resend handles your address to deliver those emails and keeps its own delivery records under its privacy policy. The list is stored with our host, Cloudflare, and a copy is kept with Resend so we can send the news. When you leave, your address is removed from both.
  • Stopping floods. To stop someone filling a stranger’s inbox, we count how many confirmation emails went to an address each day, using a one-way scrambled version of the address. Those counts are deleted within two days.

What our host sees

This site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical information, such as an IP address and the page requested, to deliver the pages and keep the site secure. We do not use that information to identify visitors. Cloudflare's dashboard also shows us simple totals, such as how many pages were requested. We use those totals to see whether the site is working and growing, not to follow individual people. You can read Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

Children

DUDE & DADDY is a father-and-son studio, and our games are meant for families. The news list is for grown-ups only, and this website does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has joined the list or sent us personal information, please let us know and we will delete it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or want something removed? Email hello@dudeanddaddy.com.

Our game

This page covers only dudeanddaddy.com. Our game, Clean Up On Aisle Scream!, is a separate website with its own features, such as player nicknames on online leaderboards and online co-op play. Its privacy notice is at cleanuponaislescream.com/privacy.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if our site adds accounts, we will update this page before we collect anything new. (November 2026: we added the news list.)
