Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026.

The short version

This website does not ask for, collect, or sell personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, no comments, and no chat. We do not use advertising trackers.

What the site stores

  • Your light or dark choice. If you tap the Day shift / Night shift button, your choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device.
  • Fonts. The fonts are served from this site, not from a font company.

What our host sees

This site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical information, such as an IP address and the page requested, to deliver the pages and keep the site secure. We do not use that information to identify visitors. You can read Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

Children

DUDE & DADDY is a father-and-son studio, and our games are meant for families. This website does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please let us know and we will delete it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or want something removed? Email robewise@gmail.com.

Changes

Our game

This page covers only dudeanddaddy.com. Our game, Clean Up On Aisle Scream!, is a separate website with its own features, such as player nicknames on online leaderboards and online co-op play. Its privacy notice is at cleanuponaislescream.com/privacy.

If this ever changes, for example when our site adds accounts or sign-ups, we will update this page before we collect anything.
